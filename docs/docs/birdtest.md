# BirdMathTest

A page for checking that LaTeX math renders correctly.

## Inline math

The Pythagorean theorem states that $a^2 + b^2 = c^2$ for a right-angled triangle with legs $a$, $b$ and hypotenuse $c$.

Euler's identity, $e^{i\pi} + 1 = 0$, links five fundamental constants.

## Display math

The Gaussian integral:

$$\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} e^{-x^2}\, dx = \sqrt{\pi}$$

A matrix-vector equation:

$$
\begin{bmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} x \\ y \end{bmatrix}
=
\begin{bmatrix} ax + by \\ cx + dy \end{bmatrix}
$$
